Offline-first pilot logbook

Tarmac Log — the pilot logbook that works without a signal

Your logbook shouldn't need a signal. Log flights on the ramp, in the air, anywhere — Tarmac Log saves instantly on your device and syncs itself when you're back online.

Available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. Free to download.

Features

A pilot logbook that works where you actually fly

Hangars have thick walls. Aprons have no signal. Tarmac Log keeps working the moment you lose a bar.

Works without a signal

Every flight is written to your device first and synced afterwards, so the app behaves the same in the air, in a hangar or on the taxiway. No spinners. No “couldn't save”.

Log a flight while the engine cools

Route, times, landings, approaches and crew in one form. Aircraft you fly often are one tap away, and totals update as you type.

Your fleet, recognisable at a glance

Every aircraft carries a low-poly icon matched to what it is — single, twin, turboprop, jet or helicopter — so you can pick the right airframe without reading a registration.

SACAA-format PDF export

Generate an A4 landscape PDF for any date range, with a cover page, the full column-by-column spread and totals carried forward across pages.

Totals and currency without the arithmetic

Hours by aircraft type, PIC, dual, night, instrument and cross-country are kept current as you log. Set your primary authority and the breakdowns follow its rules.

Syncs privately across your devices

Your logbook syncs privately to your account and restores on any device you sign in to. It is your record of your flying — not a marketing list.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, editing and browsing all work with no connection. Changes are saved on your device and sync themselves once you're back online. Signing in and the first sync need a connection.

Which phones does it run on?

Tarmac Log runs on iPhone and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.

Can I export my logbook?

Yes, with Pro. Tarmac Log generates a SACAA-format A4 landscape PDF for any date range, with a cover page, the full column-by-column spread and totals carried forward across pages. Share it straight to email, print or cloud storage.

Is it free?

Tarmac Log is free to download. The free plan lets you log up to 3 flights and set up your aircraft, crew and airports. Pro is an optional subscription, purchased in the app, that adds unlimited flights and SACAA-format PDF logbook export.

Where is my data stored?

Every flight is saved on your device first, then synced privately to your account when you're online, so your logbook restores on any device you sign in to.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Settings. Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete Account page.
